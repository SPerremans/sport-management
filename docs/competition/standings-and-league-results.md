# League standings and calendar results

Open a league to see **Standings** first. Every registered team appears, including teams without results. Rows contain the poule, position, competition points, played, won, drawn, lost and score for/against. If the federation enables individual games, the table also shows sets (the source's “Games” column) and rally points. The Fixtures tab, competition overview, My club competitions and fixture detail show the score and named winner, or a draw. Provisional and disputed scores are identified; a pending fixture is not presented as a completed 0–0 draw.

Competition points display as whole numbers in standings, exports, settings, adjustments and fixture summaries. Saving fractional league awards or federation point adjustments is rejected. Existing decimal storage is retained.

The league header's **Results and scoring settings** opens the league's win/draw/loss points, points per fixture or per individual game, and optional game breakdown. All poules inherit the policy. Format templates continue to define sport-specific slots, sets and review windows; their win/draw/loss inputs are no longer shown. Unconfigured existing leagues retain their format's defaults until explicitly configured.

Football supports a direct home/away fixture score without showing a game table or requiring artificial slots. A federation can enable games and assign a format before registration starts. Changing the result structure after registration is refused to protect existing results. League-management permission is checked on the server. Changes to a started league additionally require override permission and a reason. Direct score entry keeps the official/override, revision, submission and review guards.

Accepted, federation-final and published source results count in standings. Draft, provisional and disputed results do not. Official winner overrides and point adjustments apply. Sorting is points, score difference, set difference, rally-point difference, then team name; ranks restart in each poule. Fixtures between different poules are not counted. Player rankings and federation-specific head-to-head tie-breaks are outside this change.

The national example awards one point per individual game won: three wins can produce 22 points. Regional badminton retains the captured fixture policy; football uses 3/1/0 per fixture. Source sheets now retain the provider's credited sets and rallies independently of raw scores. Olve 1–DZ99's retirement at 3–4 remains 3–4 in the game detail, while the standings include its awarded sets and points. Future withdrawals count only if the provider's match-statistics sheet counts that fixture. Edited source sheets do not reuse the old credited totals after their revision changes.

`CompetitionStandings.java` contains the pure aggregation rules. The match service checks scope, retrieves a league's entries, poules, registrations and contest totals in batches, then builds read-only transient `MatchRow` projections. The calculated `CompetitionFixture.ResultSummary` callback is read-only: it does not take the mutation claim, create registrations or expose private lineup/captain details. Existing lineup privacy guards remain in place.

## Competition overview filters and compact fixture rows

The Leagues tab shows one stored `CompetitionLeague.Sport` and a contains filter. Published fixtures can be filtered by that league sport and by one free-text club query. The club query combines `HomeName` and `AwayName` with OR; the sport filter combines with the club query with AND. These database-backed filters apply across the dataset, not just the visible page.

In Published fixtures, the winning home/away name is bold and the Result cell omits the repeated winner name. Draw, provisional and disputed qualifiers remain. Unplayed results use a dash. The redundant Published column is removed. The former Issue cell shows a compact symbol: check mark for a result, clock for provisional, warning for disputes/issues, flag for awarded results and dash for pending. Opening the fixture retains the full issue and result details.

`LeagueSports` initializes a new league's sport from its format, falling back to its federation, and rejects a format that conflicts with an already assigned sport. Clients can read the sport but cannot overwrite it directly. The sample generator adds `features:league-sport-filters-v1` once to fresh or existing batches. It fills only empty sport values on manifest-owned leagues, preserving configured sports and all existing names, schedules and results. It uses the five existing sport scenarios and the public fixtures, including fixtures where the same club is home and away, draws, pending, provisional and disputed results. No persistent business type, association or additional sample record was added; existing ownership and cleanup coverage remain applicable.
